The figure is a **pentagram** — technically the {5/2} star polygon: take five points arranged in a circle and connect every *second* vertex instead of adjacent ones. That's exactly the property you're describing — each of the five vertices sits at the tip of a triangular "point," and the five chords crossing through the middle carve out a small pentagon at the center as a byproduct of the construction. (The pentagon-plus-circle in your current nav logo is actually just a pentagon — no star, no triangles. What you're picturing is the true pentagram.)

Since it's the load-bearing symbol for the pentadic architecture, I gave it a proper geometric construction (five points at exact 72° intervals, connected 0→2→4→1→3→0) rather than a decorative approximation, and made it the dominant visual: a large centered pentagram in the hero, with each vertex labeled by its pillar (Music, Statistics, Adaptation, Regulation, Technology) and colored to match your existing accents.
